ABOUT DJ JESSE B

More than just
playing music.

A great DJ knows what to play, when to play it, and how to read the room.

I’ve been entertaining audiences since 1993, and more than three decades behind the DJ booth have taught me that every crowd is different.

My approach combines careful planning with the ability to read the room in real time—building energy, creating smooth transitions, and finding the music that fits the moment. That’s what DJ Jesse B. is about.

We’ll plan the important moments together. Then I’ll bring the experience, the music, and the flexibility to keep your celebration feeling natural.
